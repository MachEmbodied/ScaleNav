ScaleNav selected clips and navigation intervals, resampled video to 4 Hz and
512 x 288 pixels, and added navigation instructions, paraphrases,
and fixed training/validation splits. This release preserves the prepared
training MP4 files without another video encoding pass. Frame intervals are
zero-based and end-exclusive on the prepared 4 Hz timeline. Each archive's
annotations.jsonl retains upstream clip IDs, archive paths and source-video
paths. The release index additionally records video hashes and frame counts.
